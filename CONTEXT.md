# Context

Glossary for this repo. Three words mean "a picture" here and they are not
interchangeable.

## plate

A still captured from the running app and committed for use in documentation.
Lives in `docs/readme/plates/`. Never part of the product — the site never loads
one. Named for the printing metaphor the project already runs on: a plate is the
thing you print *from*, a fixed impression taken off a live press.

## frame

One iteration of the render loop. Qualified constantly and always in this sense:
*rest frame* (the bit-identical output at zero velocity), *impact frame* (the
oversized beat a jaw-drop owns), *painted frame* (what the mounted-issue window
deliberately lags by on a deep jump), and the 12 fps *stepped frames* world
animation is quantized to.

Never means a documentation image. See [plate](#plate).

## panel

In-scene geometry: a quad standing in the 3D world holding comic art. `ArtPanel`
is the component; `panel-wipe` and `panel-portal` are gutters that operate on
one; "a live three-panel composite" is three of them.

Never means a documentation image, and never means one iteration of the render
loop.
